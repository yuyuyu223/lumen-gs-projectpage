What can LUMEN-GS do?

LUMEN-GS is the first feed-forward reconstruction model supporting light disentanglement and intrinsic properties prediction for driving scenes. It is a deep integration of CV (computer vision) and CG (computer graphics).

Given single-frame multi-view images and the corresponding GPS/IMU data, our model predicts the intrinsic primitives and environmental light context of the scene. By further adjusting the lighting parameters, the model can support various downstream tasks.

Method

Overview of LUMEN-GS. LUMEN-GS is a feed-forward reconstruction model with dual rendering branches. It further introduces a teacher model for physical properties, enabling feed-forward intrinsic property prediction to learn from both teacher priors and physics-based constraints. The model comprises spatial feature and light context aggregation, geometric and intrinsic properties decoding, and teacher-guided dual-branch rendering supervision.
